Q4 — ADDER / SUBTRACTOR

CALCULATE
B→⊕ M→ADDERY=B⊕M, Cin=M
1M=0 → A+B.
2M=1 → A−B.
3Every B bit becomes B⊕M and Cin=M.
4M=0: B⊕0=B, Cin=0.
5M=1: B⊕1=B′, Cin=1, so A+B′+1=A−B.
6Overflow: carry into MSB differs from carry out of MSB.
🧠 Trigger: Overflow: carry into MSB differs from carry out of MSB.

BUILD / RECOGNIZE

1000→0100→0010→0001

1Ring counter: one 1 circulates. n flip-flops → n states.

3Johnson counter: inverted feedback. n flip-flops → 2n states.
